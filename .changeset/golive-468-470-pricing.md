Renewals: a prior line longer than the renewal term renews at the term's share of its price, typed or engine-priced, and a shorter one renews unscaled with a review note; Spawn Renewals reports both (golive #468). Revenue bands: an unpicked band on one line no longer unprices the rest of the order on screen, Confirm accepts a band picked on the line, and bands are listed by lower edge (golive #470).
