Create the gateway subscription after checkout (option E, E2). On a payment account whose type supports subscription billing (`PaymentProviderType.SupportsSubscriptionBilling`) and whose switch is on (`PaymentProvider.BillsSubscriptions`), both now read into `PaymentProviderConfig`, a checkout hands each new auto-renewing subscription to the gateway once its payment is booked and its card kept: a gateway subscription on the price flagged `UseForNewSubscriptions`, charging nothing now and first charging at 00:00 business time on the term's last day, whose id is then recorded on the subscription so Orders' renewal jobs skip it. A subscription whose latest term is still unpaid (a renewal Charge Renewals has not collected) stays with Orders until it is paid. New driver methods `CreateProviderSubscription`, `FindProviderSubscriptions`, `DescribeProviderPrice` and `LatestFirstChargeAt` refuse by default; Stripe implements them (`POST /v1/subscriptions` with `billing_cycle_anchor` and `proration_behavior=none`, with a 15-second timeout; its stub refuses), and a price whose billing period is not the term's is refused visibly. The id is recorded on a provider of its own. It never fails the sale, and every problem raises one open alert Task. The new `Orders.CreateProviderSubscriptions` action and its daily job (shipped Disabled, set to Preview and capped at 25 creations a pass) retry failures, create subscriptions whose first charge was too far out and backfill earlier buyers, searching the gateway first so none is created twice; the run fails only for a new problem. A host that turns `BillsSubscriptions` on must set that job Active and, after reading a preview, turn Preview off: until then a subscription the checkout could not hand over stays renewing in Orders.
