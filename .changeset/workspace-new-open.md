New and row double-click now work on every grid. The Pricing, Promotions and Charges & tax tabs open a blank form on New and open the record on double-click; New on the orders, payments and subscriptions grids opens a blank form for that entity, and on the fulfillment queue it starts an order. Double-clicking a fulfillment-queue line opens its order rather than an empty one.
