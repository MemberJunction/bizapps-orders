The gateway subscription sync no longer reports a false difference for an imported subscriber in its first year. Their current gateway invoice is the creation invoice (`subscription_create`), and the importer's term-1 payment already records its charge, yet the planner reported "charged … at creation" for each one (372 of 509 in a full-size import rehearsal), so a nightly preview could never show 0 differences. `PlanProviderSubscriptionSync` now checks whether the invoice's charge is booked before classifying any paid invoice that is not a renewal: a booked creation charge is in line, and an unbooked one (the gateway charging on top of checkout) is still reported. A booked charge now means one recorded on a payment that did not fail and sits on THIS subscription's orders (`BookedChargeFilter`, also used for renewals); before, any payment anywhere recording the charge counted. A one-off invoice whose charge someone booked by hand no longer raises `GatewayChargeNotBooked`; a proration still raises `GatewayPlanChanged`, worded "the charge is booked, but the plan changed: <old> → <new>" when it is booked.
