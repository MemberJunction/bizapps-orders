A term-following entitlement grant now covers its term's days in full in the business time zone: from 00:00 business time on the first day to 00:00 business time on the day after the last (`TermGrantWindow`). It used to end at 00:00 UTC on the last day, which cut that day short by the zone's offset (6 pm the evening before in Chicago) and, with a gateway that charges the renewal at 00:00 business time on the last day, locked a renewing customer out for hours. Booking, term extensions and term shifts write the new window, and `EvaluateGrantAccess` reads a term's dates and a cancelled subscription's end the same way (it now takes the business zone), so `CheckEntitlement` and the stored grant agree; existing grants keep their stored end until next written. A cancel whose access ends in the future moves its standing grants' `ValidTo` to that end instead of leaving the old one. `GrantStatusChanged` now also fires when a save changes a grant's `ValidFrom` or `ValidTo`, with `ChangedFields`, `FromValidFrom` and `FromValidTo` in the payload, so a downstream copy of access hears extensions, shifts and moved ends. A dead-lettered outbound delivery raises an alert Task, and every Orders alert Task is assigned to the active holders of the role named by the new `AlertTaskRole` setting (unset, alerts stay unassigned as before).
