Products, Price Lists, Subscriptions and Subscription Terms forms show the same side-rail items as before MJ 6.2. The Products header now holds all the generated-section claims, and Promotions, Accounting, Fulfillment, Lifecycle, Subscriptions, Volume simulator and Deferred Revenue mount after related, so each keeps its own rail item after Details.
