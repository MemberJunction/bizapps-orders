Once a payment gateway bills a subscription, no Orders door changes it unseen (option E, E2b). `Orders.CancelSubscription` now reads a gateway-billed subscription at the gateway first (new driver method `DescribeProviderSubscription`, refusing by default; Stripe reads `GET /v1/subscriptions/:id`): a cancel after the gateway started a billing period Orders has not booked (its renewal at 00:00 on the term's last day) is refused with nothing changed, so the renewal it charged is refunded at the gateway rather than kept, and a cancel the gateway already completed is recorded, after the term too. Otherwise it cancels at the gateway first, before its transaction, and locks the subscription row so two simultaneous cancels cannot book two credit memos: the gateway must charge nothing after coverage ends (now, at its period end, or at that instant), and if it does not confirm nothing is written. New driver method `CancelProviderSubscription` refuses by default; Stripe implements it (reads the subscription, then `DELETE` or `cancel_at_period_end` / `cancel_at` with `proration_behavior=none`, verifying the answer; its stub refuses). The cancel output gains `Gateway`, the cancel panel says the gateway bills the subscription and previews what it will do, and the `Canceled` event records what the gateway did. A cancel is refused while an E2 alert says a gateway subscription may be billing unrecorded, when those alerts cannot be read for a gateway-billed subscription, and when no open term covers the request day. The new shared `SubscriptionEntity` refuses any other change to a gateway-billed subscription's billing columns on every tier (`PROVIDER_BILLED_FIELDS`), and refuses a gateway id typed onto an existing subscription (creating one with its id stays open, for importers), sanctioned server-side for the writes that told the gateway. An order can no longer extend, renew or reactivate a gateway-billed subscription; a cancelled one bought again starts fresh with a new gateway subscription, found by the checkout through its new term and created under a new idempotency key. A return of its sale and a Duration, Shift, Seats or amount-change concession on its term are refused. The hand-over stops a gateway subscription made for one cancelled meanwhile, never records one the subscription already cancelled, and searches again after every create (two live raise an urgent `AmbiguousAtGateway`). The checkout's returning-buyer refusal includes Paused subscriptions, and the daily job's preview now searches the gateway read-only.
