Once a payment gateway bills a subscription, no Orders door changes it unseen (option E, E2b). `Orders.CancelSubscription` now cancels a gateway-billed subscription at the gateway first, before its transaction: the gateway must charge nothing after coverage ends (now, at its period end, or at that instant), and if it does not confirm nothing is written. New driver method `CancelProviderSubscription` refuses by default; Stripe implements it (reads the subscription, then `DELETE` or `cancel_at_period_end` / `cancel_at` with `proration_behavior=none`, verifying the answer; its stub refuses). The cancel output gains `Gateway`, the cancel panel says the gateway bills the subscription and previews what it will do, and the `Canceled` event records what the gateway did. A cancel is refused while an E2 alert says a gateway subscription may be billing unrecorded, and when no open term covers the request day. The new shared `SubscriptionEntity` refuses any other change to a gateway-billed subscription's billing columns on every tier (`PROVIDER_BILLED_FIELDS`; linking stays open), sanctioned server-side for the writes that told the gateway. An order can no longer extend, renew or reactivate a gateway-billed subscription; a cancelled one bought again starts fresh with a new gateway subscription. A return of its sale and a Duration, Shift, Seats or amount-change concession on its term are refused. The hand-over stops a gateway subscription made for one cancelled meanwhile, never records one the subscription already cancelled, and searches again after every create (two live raise an urgent `AmbiguousAtGateway`). The checkout's returning-buyer refusal includes Paused subscriptions, and the daily job's preview now searches the gateway read-only.
