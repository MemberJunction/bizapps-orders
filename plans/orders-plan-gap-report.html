1. Executive summary

The commercial engine is largely built. Booking, pricing, promotions, charges/tax, payments (including intercompany legs and account credit), subscriptions (create/extend/cancel/renew), returns, entitlements, gift-card issuance, fulfillment ops, and document render/send all have server code and integration checks. Master-plan §18 still says “NOW = per-line booking.” That sentence is historical. The later note in next-after-custom-forms.md is the honest one: the engine is ahead of the UI.

~engineShipped. Remaining work is named, often deliberately unfixed.
~UXOne Order Header form is live. The thesis (live preview + confirm pre-flight + org-first parties) is not.
LXPNot launch-ready. Stub Stripe, no Origin, no grant-poll job, no dry-run gate.
DocsREADME, §18, and orders-ux.md still describe a world that was retired.
shipped partial / thin UI gap vs plan deferred / out of scope / needs ruling plan text is stale
The single biggest mismatch: orders-ux.md still opens with “Status: DESIGN. Nothing is built in Angular until the mockups are signed off.” The Explorer app has four job-based sections, dashboards, worklists, a custom Order Header form with Confirm as a verb, catalog/payment/sub heroes, and header+overview panels on several entities. The mockups are no longer the build gate. They are now a backlog of UX quality the live form has not absorbed.
