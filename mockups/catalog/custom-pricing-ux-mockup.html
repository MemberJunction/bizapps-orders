Option 1: Channel Tabs & Instant Tier Ladder (Recommended)

Philosophy: Groups pricing by commercial channel (Direct / Wholesale / Partner / Government). Inside each channel, volume tiers are represented as a sequential ladder where adding a new bracket automatically computes the starting minimum quantity (`prev_max + 1`).
Key Advantage: 1-click bracket creation, zero modal popups, prevents quantity gaps/overlaps, and gives instant visual feedback via a step diagram.

Philosophy: Visual tiered card system with an embedded interactive test calculator pane, allowing pricing managers to preview order quoting calculations directly beside tier rules.
